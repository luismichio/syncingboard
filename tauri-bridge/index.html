  1. Open Figma Desktop and launch the SyncingBoard Companion plugin.
  2. In Miro, open Settings and click COPY ID next to your Pairing ID.
  3. Paste the Pairing ID into the Figma Companion and click CONNECT.
  4. The Companion status will switch to CONNECTED (LOCAL BRIDGE).
  5. Select any frame in Figma and click Detect Selection in Miro to link and sync with zero cloud latency.
  1. Open Penpot in your browser and run the SyncingBoard Companion plugin.
  2. Copy your Pairing ID from Miro Settings and paste it into the Penpot Companion.
  3. Click CONNECT to link over SyncingBridge.
  4. Select any artboard and click Detect Selection or Import in Miro.
